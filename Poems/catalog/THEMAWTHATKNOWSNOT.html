THE MAW THAT KNOWS NOT

A stone rolls between molars

Tripping and betraying words

That are dented and bent

They sound sharp, to be avoided,

And leave blood smeared on the tongue,

Drawn from those nearby

The stone rolls without tasting;

Singed ever darker, and red.

Then, round.

Ishmael
