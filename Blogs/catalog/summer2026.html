Summer, 2026

The summer solstice represents the beginning of the most abundant time of the year, and the end of the Spring's many firsts and beginnings.

This year it also coincides with the first installment of a new schedule I plan to follow for sharing my life and my art. I have spent the Spring and Winter stockpiling and organizing all of my writing, constructing this new website, and thinking about what it is I want to get out of blogging, or otherwise sharing my work online. And through those quiet, cool weather activities, I have settled on this set of desires: Four major updates per year, one as each season begins, with summaries of new developments in my life, creative projects finished and ongoing, and any topics seizing my mind at the time; as well as an annual podcast, a recording of a conversation with the people closest to me. I anticipate continuing to do small updates whenever the urge strikes me, likely in the form of movie and book reviews, or select poems I've read or written, which will be found in boxes on the right side of this page (unsure where it will end up being on the mobile version).
