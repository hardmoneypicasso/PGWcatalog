Ishmael

by Daniel Quinn

| 4.5 / 5

Culture and modern history are defined by consumption and control, but the gorilla teacher is right! There's another way. This novel is compelling throughout, even in moments where the format can become a little repetitive. I found it challenging and enlightening, and would absolutely recommend it to anyone. Especially those who feel hopeless about the future.
